Prop firm tax estimate · Utah · 2026estimate only, not tax advice

Real payouts from prop firms usually come as contractor income (a 1099), so you owe self-employment tax on top of income tax, and nothing is withheld. Type your real numbers; nothing here is saved to the cloud.

Uses 2026 IRS brackets and standard deduction, 15.3% self-employment tax on 92.35% of profit (Social Security part capped at the 2026 wage base), half of it deducted, and Utah's flat 4.45% before credits. It skips credits, the QBI deduction and other details, so check with a tax pro before filing.
